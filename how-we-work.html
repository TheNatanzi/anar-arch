The five steps

  1. 01 · Inquiry

    Write to us, or call.

    درخواست

    Tell us about your family and your dates. We reply within one business day.

  2. 02 · Consultation

    First, we hear your priorities.

    مشاوره

    We meet your family, greet the elders first, and listen. We ask how your family observes each tradition.

    • Guest needs: seating, mobility and quiet-space needs for the elders and guests, planned from this meeting on.
    • Parent involvement: the couple tells us who joins each meeting and how much the parents decide, and we keep to it.
  3. 03 · Proposal

    Here is the plan, the price, and what is included.

    پیشنهاد

    A short proposal with the rituals in order, the days, the price and exactly what is included: the events covered, the staffing, the hours and the fees, before anything is booked.

    • Decision approvals: together, we agree who approves decisions, who approves spending, and which updates each family receives.
    • Parents' pages in the language agreed in your proposal; translation and spoken support confirmed before booking.
    • Date reservation: a signed proposal and the deposit hold your dates; an inquiry or consultation does not.
  4. 04 · Planning

    Ritual by ritual, as your family keeps it.

    برنامه‌ریزی

    We plan each ceremony as your family keeps it: the ceremonies your family chooses, in the order agreed with your family. A short written summary follows every meeting, in English, with the parents' page in the language agreed in your proposal. Parents and elders included at the level you choose.

  5. 05 · The wedding days

    Your ceremonies are ready. Your family is welcomed.

    روزهای عروسی

    One timeline, called with the venue and every vendor. Elders' seats held, the officiant and family cued, every sign named in your proposal coordinated with your printer and the venue. When a sofreh is chosen, for example, the spread is checked against the sofreh key (a one-page guide naming each item on the spread, in the languages named in your proposal) before the elders are seated. Our staff are easy to find for every event and hour we run. You only have to be there. Afterwards: the thank-you card, as named in your proposal, the files handed over, and one last call to close the plan.

Questions families ask

Seven answers

Our two families come from different cultures. Can you plan both?

Yes. Persian, Afghan and South Asian weddings are what we do, and mixed-heritage couples are welcome. We blend cultures across a multi-day celebration without diluting either: each ritual stays as that family keeps it.

Do you plan the whole wedding, or only run the day?

Both. Full planning begins once your proposal is signed and runs to the thank-you; the first conversation is free. Wedding-day coordination begins 6–8 weeks before the first event: you have planned already, we run the days you have planned. We say which fits on the first call, with the price and what is included.

How early should we write?

As soon as you have a season in mind. Multi-day weddings fill venues and vendors early, and the best dates near Nowruz go first. Writing early costs nothing; we reply within one business day.

Will you work with our parents directly?

Yes. Parents and elders included at the level you choose. Elders are greeted first and asked, not told. The couple sets the level of parent involvement. Parents' pages in the language agreed in your proposal; translation and spoken support confirmed before booking.

Which languages do you plan in?

Planning runs in English. Parents' pages in the language agreed in your proposal (Persian, Dari, Pashto, Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi or another you name); translation and spoken support confirmed before booking. For calls and meetings in another language, tell us which one.

Where do you work?

The San Francisco Bay Area: the city, the East Bay and the Peninsula, the South Bay and Silicon Valley, and Wine Country (Napa and Sonoma). For a wedding further afield, write to us and we will tell you plainly whether we can.

What does it cost?

Every proposal is priced to the events, days and staffing it covers. Full planning and wedding-day coordination are quoted after the first conversation.
