Weddings

عروسی

Our celebrations are built on centuries of poetry, hospitality and sacred tradition, and they deserve more than a cookie-cutter approach. We honor the deep soul of your heritage while stripping away the stress and the outdated tropes.

Sofreh Aghd

سفرهٔ عقد

The spread at the head of the room: mirror, candlesticks, honey, bread, coins, the pomegranate.

We plan the sofreh with your family and your sofreh designer, and check the spread against the sofreh key before the elders are seated.

Every item is named in both languages, so guests from the other family learn each one at a glance.

Nikah

نکاح

The marriage contract, read before witnesses and both families.

We cue the officiant and the family, hold the elders' seats, and keep the room quiet and steady.

A one-line programme explains the ritual, so every guest can follow.

Mehndi & Sangeet

حنابندان

The henna night: music, dance and henna on the bride's hands.

The sangeet, where both families sing and dance, and the baraat that arrives with music.

High energy, run to the minute, with the elders' blessing first.

Reception & Walima

جشن عروسی · ولیمه

Welcome sign, seating, menu and table numbers in both digit sets.

The timeline called with the venue and every vendor, from the first dance to the 2 AM dance floor.

A favour box for each family at the send-off, and a thank-you card that follows.

Nowruz & Yalda

نوروز · شب یلدا

Spring, and the longest night. If your date falls near Nowruz or Shab-e Yalda, we fold the haft-sin or the pomegranate table into the celebration, in the season's own colours.

Two heritages at one table? We blend cultures across a multi-day celebration without diluting either.
